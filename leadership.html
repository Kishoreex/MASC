FOUNDER & CHAIRMAN

Dr. Ln. S. Peter

One of the early pioneers of education in Tamil Nadu, Dr. Ln. S. Peter began his journey in education with the establishment of an engineering college in 1995 under the Soosaiya Peter Educational Trust. The group has since grown to include institutions spanning Dentistry, Physiotherapy, Nursing, Management, Arts and Sciences, and schools across Chennai.

VICE-CHAIRPERSON

Ms. Mercy Florence Peter

An engineer by profession, Ms. Mercy Florence Peter completed her undergraduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a postgraduate degree (MS) in Management Information Systems at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, USA.

03 / 03
MANAGING DIRECTOR

Mr. Ajay Ravindra Kumar

Mr. Ajay Ravindra Kumar worked with the engineering team at Tesla, Inc. in California on the Roadster, Model S and Model X programmes. He holds an engineering degree, an MS in Information Technology Management from the University of Texas at Dallas, and an LLB from The Tamil Nadu Dr. Ambedkar Law University.
