Madha Group

Growing through education

Madha Arts and Science College is part of the Madha Group of Educational Institutions, founded by Dr. Ln. S. Peter, an educationalist and philanthropist.

The group has established institutions across Engineering, Dentistry, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Management, Teacher Training and schools under the Soosaiya Peter Educational Trust and the Loordu Ammal Educational Trust.
