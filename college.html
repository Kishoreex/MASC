About the College

A premier institution affiliated to the University of Madras, providing wholesome education to the student community since 2006–07.

Madha Arts & Science College

Two decades of wholesome education in Chennai

Madha Arts and Science College is a premier institution affiliated to the University of Madras and located in Chennai, providing wholesome education to the student community.

The institution was started in the year 2006–07 and has developed into an intellectual centre of academic excellence. The college offers excellent facilities and infrastructure, coupled with experienced faculty who are committed to giving every student an outstanding academic experience.

It remains deeply rooted in the mission of nurturing confident, compassionate graduates who are ready to contribute meaningfully to society.

Mission statement

Mission statement

We embody the values of compassion & excellence, reflected in empathy, care, social outreach, passion and quality.

Our mission is to weave together, from the diverse fabric of society, an ensemble of graduates and postgraduates whose qualities of integrity and leadership propel them to administer the highest levels of professionalism — so as to become high achievers and responsible citizens.

Campus & learning

A vibrant campus built for growth

Madha Arts and Science College offers students a focused academic environment supported by well-planned infrastructure, experienced faculty and a strong culture of participation.

  • Modern classrooms designed for active learning and discussion.
  • Academic support through faculty mentoring and student guidance.
  • Student-centred culture that encourages leadership, service and confidence.
